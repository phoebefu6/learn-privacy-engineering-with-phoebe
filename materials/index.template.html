Leader track 🧭 · four sessions, no code

For the data protection officer, the head of data and the product lead who decide whether a dataset may leave the building. What anonymous means in practice, how the release route changes the risk, which technique fits which release, and how to sign off with a number someone else can check.

Engineer track 🛠️ · eight sessions, hands-on in the browser

For the data engineers and analysts who prepare the release. Every widget computes in your browser on 32,561 real rows of the UCI Adult census extract: quasi-identifiers, k-anonymity, l-diversity and t-closeness, a linkage attack, Laplace noise and a privacy budget, and synthetic data checked for copies.
