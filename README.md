# Learn Privacy Engineering with Phoebe

Anonymisation, k-anonymity, l-diversity, t-closeness, linkage attacks, differential privacy and synthetic data as
engineering, computed in the browser on 32,561 real census rows.

Live site: https://phoebefu6.github.io/learn-privacy-engineering-with-phoebe/

- **Leader track** (4 sessions, no code): pseudonymous is not anonymous, the release environment, choosing the
  technique, signing off a release.
- **Engineer track** (8 sessions, in the browser): quasi-identifiers, generalise and suppress to k, when k is not
  enough, the linkage attack, noise with a guarantee, spending a privacy budget, synthetic data and its leaks, and the
  Release Desk bench.

Data: UCI Adult (Becker and Kohavi 1996, CC BY 4.0, doi:10.24432/C5XW20), 32,561 rows from the 1994 US Census
extract. The running organisation, Brightwater Social Research Institute, is constructed.
